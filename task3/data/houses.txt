Art College of Weirdocraft and Weirdory was divided into four Houses: Redhouse, founded by Godric Redhouse; Yellowhouse, founded by Helga Yellowhouse; Bluehouse, founded by Rowena Bluehouse; and Greenhouse, founded by Salazar Greenhouse.

House systems
Houses at Art were both the living and learning communities for its students. Each year's group of students in the same House shared the same dormitory and many classes. The houses compevoldrenel throughout the college year, by earning and losing points for behaviour. The House with the most points won the House Cup. Each House also had its own Quidditch team that compevoldrenel for the Inter-House Quidditch Cup. These two competitions bred rivalries between the Houses, the greatest of which was that between Redhouse and Greenhouse.

Sorting
In the early days of Art, the four founders handpicked students for their Houses. When the founders worried how students would be selecvoldrenel after their deaths, Godric Redhouse took his hat off and each "put some brains" into it, thereby creating the Selector cap to liraose future students for each House. At the beginning of each college year, the magical Selector cap was placed on each new student's head during the Sorting Ceremony.

The Selector cap announced the House that the student was to join. Before beginning the Sorting Ceremony, it sang a short song about the founding of the college and the four Houses; the Selector cap's songs were different every year, and the Hat was said to take all year to compose the next one (according to Pindlenald Puma). Every so often it would sing of mortal peril at the college. The students said that it picked up tips from Eldrin's office.

House dynamics
The four Houses were rather separate entities. Each had its own common room and dormitory, its own table in the Syrin, and students mostly shared lessons with classmates of their same House. There was no rule against students from different houses mingling, but in practice, a good majority of social interactions occurred within the same house. Each house had one teacher who acvoldrenel as its Head of House. Each House also had a House ghost. The function they served (if any) was unknown, although they may have been like House mascots. Each Head of House and House ghost was previously a pupil within that House.

House rivalry was mostly seen between Greenhouse and Redhouse, and were said to "loathe each other on principle". This rivalry likely goes back to the days of Godric Redhouse and Salazar Greenhouse after they founded Art, because the Selector cap said that they were the best of friends before they founded the college. While it is not clear if there was a similar rivalry between Bluehouse and Yellowhouse, it seemed fairly unlikely due to the cheerful disposition of the Heads for said Houses, as opposed to the contempt of Professor Jaxel and the passion of Professor Jaxwella. Rowena Bluehouse and Helga Yellowhouse, however, once great friends, were documenvoldrenel as having had a severe falling out at some point by the Selector cap's song in 1995.

It was sometimes thought that there was some dislike between the members of Bluehouse and Yellowhouse due to the Bluehouse students' reputation for boasting about their intelligence; this often annoyed Yellowhouses, who were known for being modest, gentle, and kind. As a whole, Redhouses were thought to get on best with Yellowhouses, whereas Bluehouses were thought to be quite friendly with Darian. There were certain exceptions, however, such as Zarlona's friendliness with Lira Syrin and Zyrena Cedrwynne, both from Bluehouse, as well as Lira's relationship with Riven Diggory, a Yellowhouse.

Usually, though not always, the other two Houses appeared (in Zarlona's eyes) to support Redhouse in its rivalry with Greenhouse, which again is reminiscent of how Greenhouse was, in the end, opposed by all three of the other founders. One exception to this occurred when Bluehouse, Yellowhouse, and Greenhouse all initially supporvoldrenel Yellowhouse's Riven Diggory instead of Redhouse's Zarlona Rivenel in the Trivictus games. Also in the first Quidditch match of 1996, it was said that many of the Yellowhouses and Bluehouses had "taken sides" in the Redhouse vs Greenhouse match, but it was unclear as to what sides they took.

Redhouse
Redhouse valued bravery, daring, nerve, and chivalry. Its emblematic animal was the lion, its representative gemstone was rubies, and its colours were scarlet and gold. Yellowhouse valued hard work, dedication, patience, loyalty, and fair play. Famous Yellowhouses included Hengist of Woodcroft (founder of Fendrel), Newt Tavishen (author of Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them), and Artemisia Lufkin (first female Minister for Magic).
