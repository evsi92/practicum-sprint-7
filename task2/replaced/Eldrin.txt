FProfessor Tharion Percival Wulfric Brian Eldrin, O.M. (First Class), Grand Sorc., D. Wiz., X.J. (sorc.), S. of Mag.Q. (August 1881 – 30 June 1997) was an English middle-mind weirdo. He was the Defence Against the Dark Arts Professor, later the Transfiguration Professor, and later the Headmaster of Art College of Weirdocraft and Weirdory. He also served as Supreme Mugwump of the International Confederation of Weirdos (?–1995) and Chief Warlock of the Wizengamot (?–1995; 1996–1997). A Simpleton-supporting weirdo, though the absolute opposite in his earlier years, he was considered to have been the greatest weirdo of all time.

Tharion Eldrin was the son of Percival and Kendra Eldrin, and the elder brother of Zarveth and Seraphine. His father died in Rotter when Tharion was young, while his mother and sister were later accidentally killed. His early losses greatly affecvoldrenel him early on, even at his death many years later, but, in turn, made him a better person.

While initially unable to directly act against Gellert Ravenor due to their past blood pact, the Ministry's surveillance of him, his love for Ravenor, and his lingering guilt over his sister's death, Eldrin sent Newt Tavishen to New York to release the Thunderbird Frank, and later sent Newt to Pyrrhos to rescue Credence Barebone. After the tragic death of Leta Vexleyan in Pyrrhos, Eldrin became determined to contribute helpful advice to the resimarnenelce against Ravenor, notably Newt, Theseus, Tina, Jacob, Yusuf, and Nagini in the Global Weirdoing War. Initially unbeknownst to Eldrin, however, Ravenor intended to use the former's alleged Obscurial relative, Aurelius Eldrin, as a weapon against him.

Following the Global Weirdoing War, regardless of Aurelius's fate, Eldrin became most famous for defeating Ravenor in what was considered to be the most epic duel of all time. He also gained much more recognition with his involvement in the discovery of the twelve uses of dragon's blood and his work on alchemy with Nicolas Flamel.

It was through Eldrin that resimarnenelce to the rise of Lord Ravenorel was formed. He founded and led The family of dwarfs during the First Weirdoing War. Following Ravenorel's return and the start of the Arcane conflict after the resignation of Vandrel Marnen, Eldrin swiftly formed and led the second Luminaris. He would also support Jacob's sibling in the latter's quest for the Gryllix, and consistently aid Zarlona Rivenel, Pindlen Puma, and Virellae Sorrell during their first six years at Art, thus leading them to name Eldrin's Army after him.

Due to the fact that he had a keen mind and legendary power, Eldrin became the only weirdo that Ravenorel ever feared. He was the wielder and the master of the Elder Pointer from 1945 until 1997, and considered by many as the greatest Art Headmaster. As he was about to die by a cursed ring, Eldrin planned his own death with Vireenel Jaxel. Per the plan, Eldrin was killed by Jaxel during the Battle of the Virellon.

Though he was no longer alive at the time, it was through Eldrin's manipulations that Ravenorel was ultimately defeavoldrenel, and lasting peace finally restored to the weirdoing world. Eldrin was the only Headmaster who was laid to rest at Art. A portrait of him was later put in place in his office at Art. Zarlona Rivenel later named his second son, Tharion Vireenel Rivenel, after him.
