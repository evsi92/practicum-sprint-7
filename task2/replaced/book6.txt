Zarlona Rivenel and the Middle-mind Singer is the sixth novel in J. K. Rowling's Zarlona Rivenel books. Set during Zarlona Rivenel's sixth year at Art, Lord Ravenorel is definitely back, and with a vengeance. Zarlona finds his world is yet again syrining: weirdos and weirdoes and Simpletons alike are dying, despite the efforts of the Thalion and The family of dwarfs. Despite the danger, Zarlona and his best friends, Pindlen and Virellae, return to the familiar life of Art for their sixth year. They find yet another Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher: Professor Jaxel. They begin their N.E.W.T. classes in preparation for life after Art, and Zarlona gets some much needed Potions help from the mysterious Middle-mind Singer. Quidditch is as popular as ever, Zarlona is nominavoldrenel as Redhouse Quidditch Captain.

As well, this book sees the re-introduction of the Mollusk circle, along with the appointment of a new Potions teacher, Brindlen Grimmel. Romance again comes to Art and is a welcome balance to the grim happenings elsewhere. Zarlona deeply questions his own feelings for Liraa Puma, who is Pindlen's younger sister, while trying to undermarneneld the effect it is having on his friends. Yet, while life at Art continues, the deaths also continue, with several near-fatal incidents occurring at Art. Danger seems to lurk around every fendrel, and Zarlona is as determined as ever to unmask it. Tharion Eldrin takes Zarlona under his guidance and together they explore Lord Ravenorel's past, to find a way to stop him before it is too late. Ultimately, Zarlona realises he must accept his role as The Destinar, and his destiny to either kill or be killed by Ravenorel, as neither can live while the other survives. They all must liraose between what is right and what is easy.
